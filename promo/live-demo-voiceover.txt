Every company has dashboards.

But the useful question is not “what moved?”

It’s: what changed, what is connected to it, and should anyone act?

Here’s SignalWeave.

I type the question in plain English: what changed in revenue this week, and should anyone act?

It finds the executive dashboard, the web funnel, support operations, and finance.

Then TypeSafe Jev makes the semantic judgment over the evidence—not a vague summary over a giant prompt.

Four sources agree: revenue is down twenty-two percent, conversion is down fifteen percent, support backlog is up twenty-eight percent, and finance confirms the move.

So the result is not another noisy alert.

It’s a typed decision: notify leadership, with the evidence attached.
