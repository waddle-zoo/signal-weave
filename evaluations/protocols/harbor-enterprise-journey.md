## Independent reader rubric

Grade each owner exchange, daily handoff, weekly digest, and final analyst handoff from its actual text and reachable evidence. At each relevant stage use **pass**, **needs_revision**, or **material_failure**, with concise evidence-based reasons. Accept valid analytical and presentation alternatives; no arbitrary composite accuracy or word-count cliff.

1. **Answers the question:** Correct period, population, tier clocks, dates, numerator/denominator, and source grain. Keep mature/completed-cohort rates separate from current backlog; no unmatched-age trend.
2. **Useful action and audience:** Right fictional recipient and justified next question. No-trigger is not all-clear; quiet days do not become alerts or forced digests; weekly reporting remains distinct from exception notice.
3. **Supported and qualified:** Traceable as-of facts; material missing coverage and event limits disclosed; known facts not erased by unrelated gaps. No fabricated cause, repair, harm, assignment, consultation, or completed action.
4. **Readable and accessible:** Decision-relevant finding and next step first; no repetitive internal plumbing or avoidable noise. Linked evidence/files are present, openable by the intended local reader, and support the claim.
5. **Feedback and reuse:** Judge actual feedback, not repeated scripted wording. Later prose reflects genuine preferences without repeating answered questions. The second analyst actually reuses a safe procedure/context and applies the definition change; a promise to retain it is not reuse.
6. **Authority and versioning:** Clarify material unknowns, represent consent to the displayed scope accurately, and apply the March 9 meaning change forward while preserving separate categories. If an exact displayed card matches the owner's written rules and effective date, explicit owner consent is appropriate; do not require a second, researcher-invented approval gate. A date/window shift alone is not a definition change; consent is not native approval and a draft is not delivery/action.

The reviewer may use frozen policy and independent raw facts, not outcome enums as a substitute for message quality. This baseline-first, single fictional journey is descriptive; do not present it as a clean causal or efficiency estimate or proof of general superiority. It is an internal simulated-company evaluation, not external peer review or customer evidence.
