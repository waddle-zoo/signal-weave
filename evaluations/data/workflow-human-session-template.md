# Formative human workflow session

Blank observation form for the [workflow study](../../docs/workflow-research-plan.md).
Not an executed session or a substitute for its admission/budget review. Use a
copy per consenting participant; retain recordings/identifiers privately. An LLM
role-play must be labeled simulated and excluded from real-person counts.

## Before the session

- Participant code, role and prior SignalWeave/agent/BI experience:
- Actual person or simulated owner (required):
- Consent to observation, optional recording and redacted excerpt use:
- Device/OS; installation privileges; existing agent and BI prerequisites:
- Build/release URL, executable checksum and embedded identity:
- Exact public instructions/version shown; preview versus published release:
- Company fixture/source snapshot and business clock; allowed egress:
- Treatment/baseline order and matched task assignment:
- Recruitment/assignment method; task or condition refusals and omissions:
- Source scope and policy known to the owner; questions only an expert can answer:
- Facilitator, reviewer and agreed stop/assistance rules:

Use fictional business data and private test credentials; never ask the person
to reveal a secret in a recording, transcript or research form. Do not register
into a personal agent without consent or send real messages. Setup egress and
shadow-only delivery must be understood before connecting a live provider.
An administrator-prepared source is an assisted prerequisite, not self-service
onboarding. Freeze the tested build and guide; do not silently fix them mid-task.

## Tasks and observations

Give the business objective and normal public instructions, not an exact tool
sequence, schema, ideal card or expected numerical answer. For comparisons,
both arms keep the same source/owner access, reusable notes and safe helpers.
Use different matched tasks and counterbalanced order to reduce answer learning.

| Task | What the person is trying to accomplish | Observe |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Install/connect | Install the named build, connect the supplied BI environment and agent, confirm it can read the intended scope | PATH, stale binary/process, credential handling, help/health discoverability, trust/egress understanding; record assistance |
| First answer | Ask the assigned ordinary-language business question and assess the agent's supported answer | Time to useful facts; definition/period confusion; whether evidence is understandable; unnecessary technical work |
| Review/repeat | Agree what matters, what should happen, and to whom; review the actual proposed reusable policy/source scope | Focused clarification versus overwhelming questions; ownership of unknowns; whether the person can correct the agent and understand what they approved |
| Routine/no-alert state | Check an ordinary digest or completed quiet run | Whether the check actually completed, any unrequested interruption, and whether the recipient understands silence versus a failed or unfinished run |
| Changed state | Inspect the next brief/alert after the caller advances the source and runs the workflow | What changed, why it matters, what is known versus hypothetical, appropriate recipient and next action; unnecessary interruption or missed issue |
| Partial evidence | Encounter a material gap or unresolved definition | Useful supported facts and focused next step, neither unsupported reassurance nor blanket refusal |
| Repair/stop | Correct a connection or policy and stop the repeat workflow | Findability of supported CLI/agent actions; no silent policy substitution; clear caller-owned scheduling/delivery boundary |

For each task record start/end timestamps, active effort separately from waiting,
completion/abandonment, errors, restarts and exact facilitator interventions.
Keep perceived helpfulness separate from observed effort; do not treat a person's
estimated counterfactual time savings as measured acceleration. Record overlapping
work/waiting rather than counting the same active minute twice.
Pause rules, timeouts and assistance must be fixed before the session. Preserve
the original failure when trying a repair; do not relabel the repaired attempt.
Device installation, agent onboarding and later message quality are separate
results. No study-owned scheduler or compulsory report-publication step.

## Recipient comprehension, before explanation

Ask the recipient to describe in their own words:

1. What happened, over which population and period?
2. What, if anything, needs action? What remains uncertain?
3. Who should do what next, and where is the supporting evidence?
4. Was this interruption useful? What would they remove or need clarified?

Store the actual response and rationale, not just a favorable rating. Compare
material factual claims independently with the source/reference and owner intent.
Two internal reviewers may assess outputs, but their agreement is not human
comprehension, external peer review or a customer adoption estimate.

## Closeout

- Actual output/receipt/shadow-message references and redacted participant quotes:
- Task outcomes, assistance, unknown measurements and first supported divergence:
- Product friction versus source/expert prerequisite versus experiment defect:
- Uninstall/cleanup performed with consent; test registrations/credentials removed:
- Participant corrections or withdrawal of excerpt consent:

Report all attempted sessions and assistance. A small formative panel discovers
friction; repeated tasks from one person are not independent users. Do not pool
these observations with synthetic agent episodes or software-test totals.
