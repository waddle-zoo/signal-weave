# Does a maintained card help another caller?

Status: **planned, not executed or proven**. No new product abstraction is
proposed. This follows the [completed native-query journey](evidence/query-source-onboarding-2026-10-18.md):
both SignalWeave and retained agent-authored scripts produced correct recurring
answers. A simple threshold does not establish a SignalWeave advantage.

## The human job

A business owner changes who should investigate a finding or adds a meaningful
exception. A different authorized colleague's agent should use the current
procedure, produce a clear supported handoff, and avoid asking the original
analyst to reconstruct the work. The analytical question and working SQL stay
unchanged; this tests maintaining shared intent, not rediscovering data.

## Freeze before execution

- Select one genuine owner revision and prespecified attention and data-gap
  checkpoints. Verify that existing evidence can answer it; do not invent a new
  prerequisite merely to make the card path win. If no meaningful change is
  needed, do not manufacture complexity to justify Jev. Label generated
  counterfactuals and simulated identities honestly.
- Deliver the same owner change and authoritative company context to both
  maintainers. Preserve their actual prior work. Baseline gets its shared notes,
  scripts and all BI tools; it may update code or automate deterministically.
- SignalWeave uses a provenance-verified current native binary, existing source
  and card operations. The agent reviews/approves under explicit delegated
  authority; no fabricated human consultation or covert scope expansion.
- Use a different authorized caller for the successor step, with genuinely
  shared artifacts available in both arms. A fresh thread alone is not evidence
  of a handoff benefit. Do not hide baseline notes, manufacture forgetting or
  require extra LLM use where a saved runner suffices.
- Declare finite per-actor and whole-study limits, including maintenance and
  successor effort. Audit actual source reads and model usage, preserving failed
  attempts. Reviewer admission comes before paid execution; no retries to force
  an outcome. No real messages or schedules are activated.

## Judge the outcome, not the enum

Inspect whether the successor uses the current rule rather than obsolete notes,
answers with correct evidence and material uncertainty, and names the intended
next reviewer. Count maintenance, clarification, configuration, analytical
reconstruction and final-message work. Treat internal bookkeeping prose as
presentation overhead, not evidence of missing measurements.

If both paths succeed equally simply, report no demonstrated incremental value.
If SignalWeave helps, identify the specific avoided work and trade-offs—not an
unsupported claim that cards or Jev make all BI better. A successful small
handoff can justify transfer testing across domains; it cannot establish broad
enterprise readiness or observed human adoption by itself.
