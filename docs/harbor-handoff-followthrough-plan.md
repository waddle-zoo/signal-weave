## Acceptance: would the queue lead find it useful?

Independent internal product/data reviewers inspect the actual source and outputs:

1. Account for the original handoff cohort rather than infer recovery from a
   lower aggregate. Preserve unknowns and distinguish status from response evidence.
2. Explain current work under the existing policy without inventing a new
   resolution trigger, customer-harm claim, cause, assignment or consultation.
3. Give a concrete next operational question where the evidence cannot settle
   the interpretation. A supported partial answer can be useful.
4. Keep the main update readable; put record-level support in an actually
   present, linked file. Check that the file and its membership match the claim.
5. Retain enough source/query procedure for the next colleague and count the
   reconstruction needed. Correct output alone does not prove less owner work.

No exact wording, invented causal story or mandatory new escalation earns points.
Report both outcomes and effort. A successful pair may demonstrate useful
continuity, not a SignalWeave-specific advantage, hosted memory superiority or
enterprise readiness. The card has no domain-context bindings.
