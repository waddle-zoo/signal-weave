# Owner-reviewed bootstrap development trial v4

Status: preregistered; no live authoring or monitoring results yet. This is a
small development test, not a new enterprise readiness claim or fresh holdout.

## Question

The v3-04 author changed an owner's investigate rule into notify and omitted its
investigation route. Procedural simulated approval checked inspection and dry-run
fingerprints, not agreement with the original instructions. Clearer MCP schema
wording did not solve it. A 12-call Jev policy-verification probe then misclassified
the same kind of broadening as consistent, narrowly refusing experimental
acceptance on probability alone. Do not make that probe a production approval gate.

Can an independent **simulated owner model**, using the original supplied answers,
catch and help correct authored policy drift before monitoring? This evaluates a
caller-owned review workflow using existing product tools. No production owner
agent, automatic human-approval claim or additional Jev reasoning gate is added.

## Frozen comparison

- The same two known companies from v3-04, all periods and both arms: seed
  20261002, Kindred Helpdesk `company-4468fde576db47408a2e` and Cinder Database Cloud
  `company-9e23313593edcc9dbcae`. V3 fixture data, public instructions and strict
  scoring labels are unchanged; retained scoring ambiguities remain limitations.
- Author/monitor remains saved-login Codex Luna at low effort. Both arms now get
  an independent ephemeral Luna reviewer at high effort during setup. Baseline
  submits notes; treatment submits notes and actual card policy/delivery entries.
  Both reviewers receive the same original brief, glossary, destinations and owner
  answers—not a paraphrase selected by the author. No future snapshots, expected
  monitoring outcomes, scorer output or prior trial results are exposed.
- Review assesses instruction fidelity, not empirical validity of the underlying
  source or measurement. It does not inspect source data. Actual source permissions,
  health and simulation remain product responsibilities.
- Rejections provide bounded feedback; the author must explicitly revise. At most
  three review attempts per arm/company; unchanged accepted or rejected artifacts
  replay their decision without another paid call. Edits to notes or card invalidate
  acceptance. Reviewed policy notes are frozen during monitoring in both arms;
  their binding is rechecked before each run. This trial does not measure policy
  learning or recertification during monitoring. In-flight changes cannot receive approval. Missing/failed review
  cannot finish setup. No hidden labels decide review acceptance.
- Sixteen scheduled author/monitor episodes plus at most twelve owner-review
  invocations. Maximum 80 live Jev attempts, no harness transport retries;
  45 author tool calls and a 360-second active-response timeout per episode.
  Each owner review has a 90-second active-response timeout and two tool calls.
  These timeouts cover prompt delivery, stdout processing and process wait,
  **not** server/process startup or cleanup. They are not end-to-end wall limits;
  parent cleanup may wait for an in-flight reviewer. Full wall time, timeout scope
  and any wall overrun beyond the configured response timeout are recorded.
  Its tokens and wall time are included in
  onboarding totals; reviewer tool calls are reported separately and included
  in the total. No real external notification occurs.

Run once after tests, code/process review and freezing the revision, into exclusive
`artifacts/bootstrap-owner-reviewed-v4-01`. Interrupted, failed and budget-censored
episodes remain in the denominator. No implementation/prompt/label changes during
the experiment; no rerun solely to get a favorable stochastic outcome.

## Endpoints and decision

Report accepted/rejected reviews with feedback and costs, setup completion,
native outcome **and** recipients jointly, final agent repairs/degradations,
strict structured scoring, source reads, tools, agent/Jev tokens, measured latency
and illustrative—not billed—API-equivalent prices. Final narratives receive a
separate blind adversarial review before interpreting quality.

The development goal is all four setups completed, all six native treatment
routes jointly correct without agent corrections, and no final routing regression
relative to the baseline. Even meeting that goal would only demonstrate this
workflow on two known companies, not generalization or overall cost advantage.
No efficiency gate from earlier work is relaxed. If owner acceptance still lets
bad cards through, record it as a failed correction path, not proof that a real
human would have approved or that Jev can infer missing intent.

A successful result would justify testing different analytical families and unseen
policy variations. It would not prove perfect onboarding, arbitrary chart support,
causal diagnosis, successful external delivery or enterprise-scale reliability.
