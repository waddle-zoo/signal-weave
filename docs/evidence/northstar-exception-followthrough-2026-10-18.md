# Northstar: exception detection and investigation follow-through

Status: **three native policy branches and both successor investigation handoffs
completed and independently reviewed**. These are reference-qualified development cases, not a
holdout, customer study or general accuracy estimate.

## The existing owner request

Watch all-digital net sales (Online, Mobile, Marketplace), with the company-wide
impact bridge. Compare seven dates with the same weekdays 52 weeks earlier.
A complete decline of at least 10% goes to Analytics and Data Trust first;
missing comparison dates go to Data Trust without a sales-decline claim.
Leadership receives supported findings after investigation. Nothing is sent
or scheduled in this trial.

The [previous native journey](query-source-onboarding-2026-10-18.md) proved quiet
checks but left the two important exception branches untested. A read-only
warehouse audit found natural examples. The card, source SQL and business rule
were **not changed** to obtain them.

## What actually ran

Real local Superset 4.1.3/PostgreSQL, the same reviewed query/card and live Jev;
the baseline ran its own unchanged retained code through the community Superset
MCP. Both retained their own actual analytical artifacts. No researcher-created
SQL or reference labels were supplied to the investigation agents.

All receipts were fresh and delivery-disabled; the approved card/source stayed
unchanged. The two HTTP receipts identify the signed synthetic same-tenant
successor `analytics-successor`, rather than the original local identity.
This is authenticated continuity through the native server, not real company
SSO, per-user Superset impersonation or permission certification. Source reads
still use the configured service credentials.

Independent internal review reconciled each case's twelve period/scope totals,
84 daily-grid rows and eight fact/mart reconciliation rows with native source
measurements. The gap's grid contains missing-date placeholders, not 84 populated
observations. Date presence does not prove ingestion completeness or seasonal
equivalence; currency remains undeclared.

Three live Jev 1.13.0 requests used **21,200 input / 259 output tokens**. There
was no frontier pre-analysis for those native runs. These policy calls are not
the complete human workflow or a speed/cost comparison. Baseline also completed
all three runs without recurring inference, performing additional reconciliation.

## A decision label missed a communication defect

The baseline's history-gap brief correctly directs coverage review to Data
Trust, yet prominently reports **43.9% digital growth and 44.6% company growth
across seven-versus-six-date coverage**. Those are not validated comparable
growth findings. The frozen output is retained without repair.

Native evidence preserves the known observed sums but withholds comparable
totals/changes for incomplete windows. There is no separate native-path human
gap brief in this study, so this does **not** establish a SignalWeave human-
communication win. The actual reader-facing outcome matters, not only correct
`insufficient_data` routing.

## The investigation test

One successor per arm receives its own fresh February 19 workflow output,
retained notes/procedures and the same raw BI tools. The baseline goes first.
The task is a useful proposed handoff: explain supported channel/company impact,
look for additional evidence about why, preserve unresolved causes, and identify
the next justified Analytics/Data Trust check. A causal story is not required
when the source cannot support one.

Both produced useful, supported handoffs. Baseline checked order flags, marketing
attribution and a synthetic quality warning, keeping those distinct from causes.
Treatment added category localization: six category declines total 33,397.34
source units, with gains elsewhere reducing the digital decline to 30,852.68.
Their sums and observed-date coverage reconcile to the source. Its filtered
inventory query returned no matching rows—not proof of stockouts or universal
absence of inventory data. Both explicitly retain **cause unresolved**, propose
concrete next checks, and avoid invented consultation or leadership findings.

Treatment's extra category slice gives a more localized investigation lead,
but comes from additional frontier analysis. It cannot be attributed to
SignalWeave or its policy judgments. Repeated packet/envelope inspection,
incorrect JSON paths and local extraction repairs also added work. Review found
no source/API failure or lost policy evidence. Baseline had orders and
reconciliation already; treatment's configured primary source did not. Including
the initial attention check, each path used seven SQL queries, with different
diagnostic work—not an equal-work timing experiment.

The demonstrated mechanism is **unchanged-policy detection → authenticated
successor → useful investigation handoff**. Baseline succeeds strongly too.
This pair establishes no SignalWeave investigation-speed, cost, accuracy or ROI
advantage. It also does not establish a completed causal investigation or actual
delivery. A more specific next question can be useful without being a verified
cause, and a lower provider latency is not a faster human workflow.

The normal native server is verified as the 11-tool agent profile. This follow-up
stage admits only its six read tools—no card mutation or second evaluation—plus
the same 27 raw BI tools. Both frontier actors use Luna through Codex with
600-second/40-MCP/70-workspace-operation limits; whole-study between-actor stops
are 5 million input or 250,000 uncached input tokens. These are admission stops,
not in-flight token ceilings. Both completed with known usage: combined
4,196,100 input including cache, 279,044 uncached and 67,879 output tokens. The
second actor was admitted below the stop and finished above it; no further actor
is eligible. No additional Jev calls or real delivery occurred. Earlier known
and unknown authoring costs remain in the predecessor reports, not silently
amortized away. Actor timings exclude some process setup/cleanup and are not
complete installation-to-delivery timings.

## Adding another workflow must preserve the first

A separate onboarding regression showed that importing another query replaced
the active catalog and deactivated the first query reference after reconnect.
The repair upserts by key, preserving unmentioned queries; it warns when a changed
definition needs dependent-card review. No new workflow abstraction was added.

An independently reviewed source control used a clean native build of `7025155`
in a cloned private home. Two actual CLI imports of a second query, followed by
two normal-profile stdio reconnects, preserved the approved card and all 79
original business values. The additional `SELECT 42` source also worked in both
sessions. Four real Superset SQL reads, zero forwarded or rejected provider
requests, and no frontier agent, card evaluation or delivery were involved.
This proves source coexistence and preservation of the existing monitor's
inputs—not two approved monitors or a comparative business benefit. The two
isolated source/native skill-installation tests also passed for Codex and Claude;
they did not modify personal client configuration or test human usability.

Control binary: `715fec8d15aad9f899577fd4882881fcc45b063b8ffb3922a125addbdfa510d8`;
source fingerprint: `f689067880d9a4816ef5bbba80c9688735a70c164f870cee21e47c50e032dfe1`.
Its raw control and logs remain in ignored `artifacts/query-catalog-native-control-v1`.
The business study used the earlier binary; this repair does not rescore it.

Final offline verification passed **4,888 tests, 13 opt-in skips**, plus Ruff.
