# Published-release workflow trials — October 20–21

These trials ask whether adding SignalWeave helps a capable BI agent deliver
useful recurring analysis. They exercise fresh onboarding, retained business
context, investigations, and actual local-inbox handoffs—not just decision
labels. Results are mixed; they do not establish enterprise readiness.

## What actually ran

- The published **v0.2.0rc5** macOS native bundle, isolated homes, the terminal
  setup wizard, Codex registration, and the bundled SignalWeave skill.
- **Luna xhigh** for both analyst arms; live Jev for SignalWeave policy checks.
  Baselines retained the same company source tools, owner knowledge, workspace,
  SQL, code, and automation abilities.
- Independent source arithmetic, preserved original failures, and a separate
  reader review of closed message packets. Tooling clues make label blinding
  partial. Simulated owners and recipients are not actual customer users.

Research helpers extracted/copied the release and forwarded agent-authored
query files to its native CLI. This is not a new public-installer usability
study. Timings include the analyst's business onboarding and execution, but
exclude download, wizard/preflight, and independent review. Serial runs share
host/source/cache conditions; differences are descriptive, not causal effects.

## Northstar: fulfillment monitoring and investigation

The agents independently onboarded a weekly damage review against actual local
Superset and the same 27 community-MCP analytical tools. Seven finalized
historical cohorts included a qualifying damage spike and a final period with
missing dates. This is retrospective analysis, not an as-known-at-the-time
backtest.

| Fresh paired run | Agent only | Agent + SignalWeave |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Supported core weekly figures/routes | 7/7 | 7/7 |
| Analyst wall time | 621 seconds | 734 seconds |
| Uncached frontier input tokens | 165,089 | 298,225 |
| Physical SQLLab executions | 25 | 20 |
| Delivered local messages | 11 | 11 |
| Supporting attachments | 0 | 17 |

SignalWeave completed seven live-Jev evaluations without provider or local
quota errors. Its qualifying investigation supplied a **12-record damage
worklist**, matching every independently verified source ID; the baseline
supplied no worklist. A supplemental source check reconciled all 30 records
across five CSVs, seven weekly cohorts, 49 daily buckets, and the additional
late-rate/delivery-time claims. These are coverage units, not a global accuracy
percentage.

Both agents sent unnecessary monitoring-metadata questions. SignalWeave also
sent evaluation-process commentary to Dispatch, an audience mismatch. It was
**18.2% slower** and used more frontier tokens. Fewer queries do not establish
lower warehouse cost; the earlier paired attempt had the opposite query-count
direction.

The earlier attempt remains a separate diagnostic: the research proxy's
nine-call ceiling blocked three later requests locally. Those were not Jev
rate-limit failures, and that censored attempt is not a clean reliability or
efficiency comparison.

## Harbor: two workflows over a month

The separate Harbor study uses 28 as-of source snapshots, daily priority-ticket
handoffs, weekly support-commitment briefs, five model-simulated company roles,
and an owner policy change. A real local company MCP reads isolated SQLite
history; this is not a hosted vendor integration. Every daily cutoff contains
qualifying work, so this cohort cannot measure quiet-day suppression.

The baseline completed the month but lost the Operations destination from its
retained notes: later weekly reports were generated and delivered to the daily
queue recipients instead. Two late simulated-feedback calls hit the research
reply cap; preserve that censoring rather than claiming a clean paired result.

Both SignalWeave onboarding stages completed with live Jev. Independent review
found weekly recurring-delivery consent unfinished. One separately reviewed
simulated-owner question completed permission for local manual-review drafts,
without analytical repairs. This is **operator-assisted completion**, not
zero-touch onboarding. Its extra interaction is accounted separately. The
bounded recurring run is in progress; no paired Harbor quality verdict yet.

## Reproduction and claim boundaries

The [structured results](../../evaluations/data/workflow-product-2026-10-20.json)
record measurements, source boundaries, artifact hashes, and limitations. The
[staged protocol](../../evaluations/protocols/workflow-product-2026-10-20.json)
separates completed runs from the unexecuted new-company transfer stage.
Large traces and source snapshots stay in ignored local artifacts; their
hashes alone are not public reproduction.

No monetary savings, Jev-specific causal advantage, real-human time savings,
external Slack/email delivery, or general enterprise superiority is established.
The demonstrated value is narrower: reviewed cards can retain a policy and
produce useful recurring evidence and investigation handoffs. Onboarding
effort, recipient focus, and repeatable advantage still need work.
