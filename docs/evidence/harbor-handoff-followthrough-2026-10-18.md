# Harbor: a quiet check is not a finished investigation

Both successor agents traced the previous handoff into its later recorded state
and produced correct, linked case files without analytical rescue. SignalWeave's
update offered a clearer manual-review suggestion, but took more work. This
known-company pair establishes useful follow-through, **not overall superiority**.

## The person's question

“What happened to the cases we previously flagged, what still needs attention,
and what should the queue lead check next?”

The [earlier Harbor journey](harbor-onboarding-2026-10-17.md) had reported that the
unanswered-ticket trigger went quiet without following the previous cases. The
new task keeps that alert policy unchanged; a requested follow-up can still
deserve an answer when no new alert is justified.

  tickets. **104 response-visible open tickets** remain past their resolution
  clocks, explicitly outside the existing unanswered-only rule.
- Both provide locally openable CSVs with all 80 prior cases and all 104

- Treatment suggests a queue-lead review of the resolution list and identifies
  its most overdue case. Baseline states the unresolved issues without a crisp
  next ask. Both could more directly ask whether the earlier review occurred and
  why response events remain absent. That is an actionability limitation, not
  a reason to invent another alert policy.

The supporting files keep the long case lists out of the main message. Both
updates remain local drafts; no notification, assignment or schedule ran.

## Method, review and limits

The [trial plan](../harbor-handoff-followthrough-plan.md) and
[opt-in runner](../../evaluations/harbor_handoff_followthrough.py) preserve the
actual artifacts, source/binary identities, finite admission and failed attempts.

between-actor stops were 2.5M input and 180k uncached tokens, not in-flight caps.
Both completed: combined 1,760,572 input, 176,700 uncached and 54,018 output tokens.
No retries, new policy-provider requests, retained-input/capture modification or
card mutation occurred.

Two zero-inference preparations failed before admission: a WAL-mode backup
could not be reopened read-only, and a comparison mistook a compact approval response for the full
stored card. Repairs preserve the actual store payloads and business policy;
the failed roots/logs remain. The completed preparation checked each actor's
actual read grants, private-path/network denial, native card/receipt access and
raw/native source parity. No actor received reference findings or a researcher-
generated cohort list or transition query.
