# Harbor: follow the whole job, including the failures

**The trial delivered source-supported work, but did not complete the
planned month.** SignalWeave's agent delivered 15 daily handoffs and three
weekly briefs before a simulated-owner reply quota blocked the exact-version
v8 consent request before inference. The baseline completed 28 source checks but omitted two required
daily handoffs. Neither result establishes general superiority or readiness.

The [compact record](../../evaluations/data/harbor-whole-journey-2026-10-19-summary.json)
preserves stage usage, executable identities, artifact hashes and unrun work.
Raw traces, databases and native homes remain outside Git; the compact record
is not a publicly hosted full-transcript archive.

## What actually ran

One fictional support company, five simulated roles, two workflows and 28
dependent historical daily states. Both arms used Luna xhigh, the same actual
read-only company MCP, and their own persistent SQL, files and notes. Treatment
also received the normal native SignalWeave profile, its bundled skill and
live Jev. Simulated employees used Sol low in both arms. Baseline ran first.

Each arm had separate queue and weekly onboarding sessions, then a continuous
operating session. The agents had to discover definitions, clarify business
intent, author their own queries, investigate, deliver local inbox messages,
read replies and retain the procedure. No expert business query or card was
preloaded. Treatment's fresh native setup and reconnect were real, but scripted
operator work with an IT-provided typed MCP bridge—not a novice-human study.

The frozen [owner context](../../evaluations/data/harbor-enterprise-company-roles-v4.json)
and [protocol](../../evaluations/protocols/harbor-enterprise-journey-v4.md)
separate the unanswered-ticket rule from resolution follow-up and a weekly
resolved-ticket rate. A predeclared March 9 owner event expanded the daily
rule. It was not spontaneous reader feedback or a researcher rescue.

## What people received

| Observed work | Baseline | SignalWeave agent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Daily facts checked against raw history | 28 correct query sets | 15 source-supported delivered packages |
| Daily handoff dates | 26; March 4/5 omitted despite qualifying work | 15; last date used manual analysis, not the new card |
| Weekly briefs | Four numerically correct | Three numerically correct; fourth not reached |
| Local messages / copied attachments | 68 / 234 | 36 / 62 |
| Recorded recipient attachment opens | Zero | Zero |

Treatment's three weekly rates were **7/98, 11/90 and 17/106**, correctly scoped
to visibly resolved priority-1 tickets in the latest ended creation cohort.
Its final daily handoff distinguished **eight unanswered response-clock
breaches** from **99 responded/open resolution-clock items**, without inventing
cause, customer harm, assignment or repair. All delivered ticket IDs and
fields matched independently recomputed raw snapshots.

Correct numbers were not enough. Treatment repeated technical diagnostics in
daily messages; one correctly qualifying ticket rounded to “0.0 hours over,”
hiding its small positive margin. Its operator summary acknowledged pending
work, but no final owner-inbox handoff was sent. The baseline's final coverage
handoff did not clearly refresh a historical 0/24 gap to the final 15/24 state.
File copies were verified; recipient use of attached detail was not observed.

## Why the month stopped

An overly broad card gate initially suppressed known qualifying tickets because
current-week population coverage was unknown. The agent clarified and revised
it. Repeated per-row metadata then pushed two evaluations over the input budget;
the agent compacted its card/query and separately re-evaluated them successfully.
The runner also reused an evaluation key after a card change; native rejection
was correct, and separately keyed evaluations succeeded.

By the March 9 change, the simulated owner had used its 12 allowed replies.
The exact v8 consent request was blocked **before inference**. V8 remained a
draft, and the agent stopped rather than pretending the updated card had run.
The manual March 9 handoff was source-supported and authorized, but is not a native v8
decision. The remaining **13 daily cutoffs were not run**.

This is budget censoring plus observed onboarding friction—not an owner refusal,
unknown model spend or proof that later source decisions would fail. Baseline
separately hit a 96-attachment lifetime quota. These different constraints
prevent an unqualified cost, noise or completion comparison. Earlier failed
attempts are retained separately, not erased or pooled as successful trials.

## Reproduce and improve

The study used source commit `04f4145` and the native/skill fingerprint recorded
in the JSON, not the published preview. The frozen roles/protocol are historical
v4 inputs; later runner limits must not be applied retroactively. Generate the
fixture with `evaluations.harbor_enterprise_fixture`, then use
`evaluations.harbor_raw_reference` to recompute exact as-of source facts without
importing the generator's calculations. Follow the frozen protocol and actual
admission/preflight gates before any paid actor run.

Independent internal data and product agents reviewed actual messages, raw
facts and recipient files. This was arm-aware simulated-company review, not
external peer review. All daily states contained qualifying work: quiet-day
suppression, the unrun source-gap investigation, statistical reliability and
real Slack delivery are **not established**.

The next iteration targets compact policy evidence, claim-specific coverage,
correct new-run identities and predeclared employee capacity. Serializer tests
or a few live provider checks cannot substitute for another complete journey.

### Post-run repair controls—not a repaired journey

The first lossless metadata experiment moved shared business text as well as
wrappers. Its labels matched, but a confidence change from 0.97 to 0.76 would
change stricter routing. That candidate was rejected. The narrower candidate
keeps business values and statements inline; eight paired diagnostic labels
matched, with one remaining difference at a 0.95 confidence gate. These are
encoding controls, not eight enterprise cases or proof of probability equivalence.

The final source preserves ordinary and legacy compact requests that already
fit, factoring repeated source metadata only under continuing budget pressure.
Two retained overflows each completed in one live request, returning all 30/31
original evidence rows exactly. Across all three controls, 34 provider requests
used 98,617 input and 2,422 output tokens. The compact record retains the
counterexamples and exact final source fingerprint. No provider limit or
confidence gate was relaxed; current native and full-journey proof is separate.
The final source suite passed 5,097 tests with 13 opt-in skips, and scoped
internal code review cleared the limited change—not enterprise readiness.
