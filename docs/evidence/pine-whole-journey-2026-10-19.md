# Pine Courier: installation through three weekly reviews

SignalWeave completed a whole fictional-company workflow: fresh native setup,
source discovery, owner clarification, an agent-authored query and card,
approval, three recurring live-Jev evaluations, investigation, inbox delivery,
feedback and source-repair follow-up. Independent raw-data and product reviews
verified the delivered business findings. This establishes a working journey,
not a cheaper or more accurate agent than the capable baseline.

The [compact execution record](../../evaluations/data/pine-whole-journey-2026-10-19-summary.json)
contains the actual messages, usage, source hashes and stage verdicts. Large
transcripts, databases and binaries stay outside tracked documentation.

## What Dispatch received

The owner wanted a weekly digest of all noncancelled parcels due in each closed
UTC week, including overdue undelivered parcels. An overall attainment decline
of at least five percentage points requires investigation and a Dispatch notice.
Data Operations handles source/export problems; volume alone is not a trigger.

| Current due week | Independently verified attainment | SignalWeave-assisted agent's delivered result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| September 14–21 | 2,550/2,850 = 89.47%; unchanged | Routine digest, no exception |
| September 21–28 | 2,772/3,420 = 81.05%; down 8.42 points | Dispatch investigation notice; separate Data Operations repair request |
| September 28–October 5 | 3,486/3,990 = 87.37%; up 6.32 points | Routine recovery update; source-backed denominator follow-up |

The middle cached total lacked its denominator. The agent used matching raw
parcels, tier counts and population manifests to preserve the supported business
finding rather than refusing the whole analysis. It investigated tier and
late/undelivered contributions without inventing a cause. Later, it verified
the corrected cache row and followed the earlier parcel cohort: 123 additional
late-delivery timestamps were visible, without changing its on-time attainment.

One approved card, version 1, and the same registered SQL served all three
observations. Each evaluation used a distinct key and current source cutoff.
Live Jev returned `ignore`, `investigate`, `ignore`; the agent still delivered
the requested ordinary digests. All five company messages reached actual local
simulation inboxes. No real Slack message or schedule was created.

## The whole setup—not a supplied gold card

The actual GitHub Actions archive was checksum-verified and installed into a
fresh fake person's home. The real eight-prompt wizard stored the key, connected
the company MCP, registered with the real Codex CLI and installed the bundled
skill. Status, offline health and live source health passed. Scripted wizard
execution took 7.78 seconds; this is not measured human installation time or a
test of the published curl installer. The company supplied its initial reviewed
read-only source manifest; arbitrary MCP normalization was not automatic.

The analyst began with an empty workspace and no card or analytical SQL. It
asked the fictional owner, wrote and checked its own aggregation, requested one
actual CLI source registration, obtained approval for the displayed card and
continued without researcher-authored SQL or analytical rescue. A pre-registration
query draft was corrected by the agent itself; it is not the registered query
credited here. The baseline retained the same raw company tools, owner answers,
SQL/code/file capabilities and its own reusable procedure.

also encountered an irrelevant registration helper; that harness friction is
not a treatment benefit.

## What review caught

Independent internal reviewers replayed retained queries against all three
archives and reconstructed visible parcel events. No numerical or fabricated
causal errors were found in the five delivered treatment messages. Product
review found useful digests, correct routes and real reuse, with one practical
clarity gap: the decline notice ends with “No specific owner action is assigned”
rather than giving Dispatch an explicit next step. Baseline review separately
flagged repeated follow-ups about an unconfirmed dashboard binding.

Two more distinctions matter:

- Treatment supplied traceable source IDs, but no directly openable recipient
  attachment. The simulator's file-copy mechanism was underdocumented. The
  bodies contain supported findings; convenient evidence access is not proven.
- The final operator summary says Data Operations published the correction.
  The source verifies revision 2 and its values, not its publisher. The delivered
  company messages avoid that attribution; the operator claim remains a defect.

The bundled skill now clarifies recommended next steps versus accepted
assignments, and source corrections versus their author. That is a forward
guidance change, not a retroactive improvement to this trial. The next Northstar
journey tests the built-in Superset/query path, a different business policy and
clearer recipient-accessible evidence delivery.

## Reproduction and scope

The [whole-journey runner](../../evaluations/enterprise_full_journey.py),
[native setup helper](../../evaluations/enterprise_native_setup.py),
[public source generator](../../evaluations/pine_courier_history.py) and

private archive dependency. Message meaning still requires the separate review;
the structure grader deliberately does not infer it from labels or keywords.

skips; those are software checks, not additional business cases.

This is one company and three dependent historical observations, using actual
native/Codex/Jev execution and scripted company people. It includes no exact
five-point boundary case, statistical generalization or external peer review.
