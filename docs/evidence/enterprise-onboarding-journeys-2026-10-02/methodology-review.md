# Internal adversarial review record

This is internal agent review, not external peer review, human customer approval,
or enterprise certification. Reviewers used Luna and knew the study design and
some earlier operational failures. Narrative packets omit arm labels and private
scores, but output style and missing submissions can reveal the treatment.

## Before and during the frozen run

Reviewer Maxwell (`01a0ff31-e203-7553-8572-58c841e5f650`) reviewed the six-company
methodology. It preserved private labels and measurements, supplied no expert
cards, and retained failed episodes. The parent corrected the reviewer's initial
transport/model confusion: both arms use Luna through Codex, not different models.
The scope remains known-family regression with cloned scoped catalog alternatives.

Reviewer Dewey (`01a0ff37-6c6f-7390-b2c2-20f7593fec9b`) inspected operational
onboarding failures without changing the running code. It found:

- Source discovery reruns during proposal, so prior selected references can fall
  outside the newly ranked visible candidates.
- The author used source-reference objects without the required `ref` field.
- Harbor reached native review and synthetic owner approval, then native approval
  rejected the review fingerprint. A subsequent offline real-MCP characterization
  reproduces the boundary instability without network calls.
- The simulated owner sees policy text but not the inspected source contracts.
  Its rejections of supported source-validation/reporting steps cannot be treated
  as authoritative evidence that those steps violate company intent.

The root-cause findings do not turn failed setups into passed ones. The runtime,
approval rules, fixtures and scoring labels remained frozen throughout the run.

## Offline export review and repairs

Maxwell found that the first exporter trusted sibling trace/report files and used
unvalidated hardcoded counts. The offline exporter was strengthened before final
export to reject missing/duplicate episode rows, unknown episodes/providers,
unmatched/duplicate API attempts and report/trace usage mismatches. It reports
missing native decisions separately and marks that metric inapplicable to baseline.
It also retains failed invocation/event counts and warns about masking limits.
Ten offline exporter tests cover these contracts; this does not alter model outputs.

## Narrative-review quality control

The original narrative-review files are retained, not overwritten. The parent
found omissions in both first passes:

- Review A checked the retail arithmetic but missed a fabricated citation suffix.
  Its follow-up uses exact snapshot/ref matching and downgrades that report.
- Review B incorrectly claimed raw snapshots were unavailable, although the packet
  includes `business.period.snapshots`. The parent required re-review against those
  payloads and owner policy rather than treating the output's own assertions as
  evidence. Its follow-up is the applicable assessment.

This review correction is itself a limitation: an agent panel's initial agreement
is not sufficient proof. Structured checks, original source evidence, missing-output
denominators and inspectable reviewer reasons remain necessary. No live rerun or
private-label change was made to obtain better review results.
