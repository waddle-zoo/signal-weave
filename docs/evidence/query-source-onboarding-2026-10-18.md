# Native query onboarding: remove the custom reader

Status: **source preparation completed; the first native journey failed before
card evaluation**. Interface repairs are under evaluation. No Jev request,
completed business handoff, recurring benefit or enterprise-readiness result
is claimed by this study.

## What the user should get

An agent answers a business question, preserves its verified query and the
owner's intent, then repeats that check through SignalWeave's existing BI
connection. The owner should not build another Python reader/MCP service or
re-enter credentials. The final test is a useful report or appropriate silence,
with supported evidence and next steps—not successful import or a decision enum.

This follows the [Northstar retained-analysis journeys](northstar-workspace-first-use-2026-10-18.md),
which produced useful answers without an overall SignalWeave advantage. The
previous custom-reader authoring attempt was censored with unknown cost; it
remains unchanged and is not a completed baseline for this study.

- Local synthetic Northstar data in real Superset **4.1.3**, the unchanged
  community Superset MCP, Luna through Codex, and a newly built standalone
  SignalWeave executable. This is not downloaded-release or customer evidence.
- Clean native commit `cc13b22`, production fingerprint
  `e1911ead1eb7a220fb495f34d767a8f891fca297af2294459bc23dd595c5014b`,
  binary `8e14fb77439b1e8b2ab35eddab40dc6fd9b3dd0e8d92024fe799236303aec892`.
  A scripted fresh-home wizard completed, actual isolated Codex registration

  notes/SQL/owner definitions. It received public product guidance/schema and
  its own workspace, not researcher SQL, replacement policy or target labels.
  Raw company tools remained available; original captures were read-only.
- The expert produced one query artifact and review notes, with SQL-computed
  totals, changes, date coverage and company reconciliation. There was no
  custom reader, client SDK installation or publication refresh.

The author took **349.76 seconds**, 8 MCP calls and 36 workspace operations.
CLI usage was **2,024,328 input including 1,804,544 cached; 219,784 uncached;
38,505 output tokens**. These are not dollar costs or a complete warehouse-work
measure. This is counted onboarding effort, not free preparation.

## Good analysis did not establish a working product flow

The query preserved Online + Mobile + Marketplace, the 364-day weekday shift,
seven represented dates, channel evidence and the company bridge. Its September
18 verification matched all 24 retained comparison quantities. September 4's
source execution supported digital +18,866.77/+6.65%, company +18,665.42/+3.70%,
and a zero bridge residual. Both cutoffs were known historical checks, not an
unseen holdout. The author queried the later cutoff during preparation.

Preparation first produced a threefold digital join inflation. The expert
caught and repaired its own SQL; the failed source capture remains. Independent
internal PM/DE and method/code reviewers checked the corrected artifact and
actual source replies. No operator analytical correction occurred. This does
not prove that future exact-threshold or missing-data cases work.

After scoped source consent, the native CLI imported the exact artifact. One
zero-Jev inspection through the verified normal 11-tool profile failed. Its
research logger saved only an `ExceptionGroup`, losing the leaf error. A
separately admitted, unchanged diagnostic saved the actual error:
`SQL Lab response exceeded the byte limit`. Both source attempts and the
original logging failure remain recorded; neither was rescored as a success.

The first interface mixed transport and evidence limits: the artifact allowed
50,000 bytes, but the saved provider result was about 64 KB, mostly SQL/technical
metadata; measurement rows were about 3 KB. A second problem was already visible
in code: the expert used `source_status=unknown` to qualify an example dataset,
which would block a required source even after successful SQL retrieval.
Rounded percentages also leave exact decision-boundary behavior unproved.

**No card approval, Jev evaluation or native recurrence occurred.**

## Product changes and remaining proof

The [version-2 interface](../query-sources.md) separates small, optional business
meaning from code-owned connection health and tenant authority. It separately
bounds normalized evidence and raw SQL Lab responses, retaining stricter Preset
limits. Legacy artifacts are rejected for explicit expert migration; their
health is not silently changed to make them pass. Decision quantities should
remain unrounded; human formatting is separate.

These are generalized interface repairs, not repaired v1 results. The next
gate is expert migration/review, a new current-native inspection, exact card
review and a useful agent readout, then an unchanged fresh-cutoff run. Count
every additional step. Subsequent attention, quiet, data-gap, schema/connector
transfer and fair-baseline cases remain required. Two quiet dates cannot prove
recurring efficacy, reduced toil or comparative value.

Raw methods, frozen inputs, source replies and outcomes remain ignored under
`artifacts/northstar-native-query-journey-v1`. Reviewers are internal agents,
not customers, human study participants or external peer reviewers.
