# Blind narrative review: Retail, Workspace, Helpdesk

Evidence: only `blind-review.json` in this directory, restricted to Juniper Trail Retail, Harbor Workspace, and Kindred Helpdesk. Repository instructions were read in `AGENTS.md`. No results, mappings, traces, arm identities, or other reports were consulted. Each company has two anonymous outputs for each of three dated conditions: 18 outputs, all nonempty and marked execution complete. No arm identity is inferred. Missing or empty outputs would remain unassessed; there are none in this subset.

**Findings: 13 supported, 3 qualified, 2 unsupported.** Review-024 materially understates Retail sales by omitting shop proceeds. Review-019 withholds a required Helpdesk alert because it mistakes a comparison label difference for a missing weekly comparison. The three qualified Retail outputs have correct arithmetic, but finance closure is not affirmatively evidenced in the permitted packet. These counts describe this subset, not trial-wide accuracy or comparative arm performance.

## Review standard and evidence limits

Supported means the substantive quantities, interpretation, outcome, and destination follow the supplied evidence and owner policy. Qualified means useful, arithmetically supported content retains a material validity limitation. Unsupported means a material reported quantity or decision conflicts with the evidence; correct portions are still credited. Minor wording problems are identified separately rather than treated as arithmetic failures.

I used the public glossary, owner answers, catalog descriptions, snapshot values and contracts, `numeric_vocabulary`, and `submission_contract.numeric_definitions`. Deltas are signed current minus baseline. For rates, weights are eligible-denominator shares: mix = sum of average segment rates times weight changes; within = sum of average segment weights times rate changes. These are ratio units, not relative percentage changes. Retail web contribution is the change in web net proceeds, not current web proceeds.

**Finance closure limitation:** the selected Retail packets contain `refund_timing` and posting-policy text, but no explicit finance-close status, posting-completeness attestation, controlling total, or separately stated closure rule. The public owner policy explicitly sends incomplete postings to `insufficient_data`. `healthy`, `authorized`, current capture time, and `posting_week` establish different properties; they do not prove all postings are closed. Thus reviews 002, 013, and 017 receive qualified financial validity, not arithmetic failures. Their ignore/notify choices match the supplied rows and thresholds, conditional on complete postings. The packet does not establish that postings are incomplete either. I cannot assign an unconditional finance-closure outcome or invent an undisclosed mandatory closure field. If affirmative closure is required by an additional policy, that policy and its evidence are absent from the sole permitted source.

All submitted source references resolve to the correct company's current-period snapshots and appear in that output's `inspected_refs`. Cited snapshots are marked authorized and healthy, have no source error, and match their packet's period and capture time. Every submitted numeric fact and unit belongs to the public vocabulary and definitions. This verifies reference integrity, not automatic support for every sentence: review-024 cites the right source but misaggregates it. There is no arithmetic penalty for spelling or equivalent wording.

## Business value and limits

The supported outputs show useful bounded decisions: distinguish within-plan retention losses from mix, distinguish service deterioration from volume, and route missing eligibility or denominators to the appropriate data owner. Retail decomposition can focus investigation on web gross revenue and refunds, but does not establish why either changed or certify a financial close. None of the reviewed outputs establishes a business causal mechanism; accounting contributions and explicitly tentative hypotheses are appropriately narrower.

Chronological packet dates do not establish an unrevised longitudinal ledger. For example, Helpdesk's Oct 08 current numerator is 1,500 but the next packet's baseline for that interval is 900; Workspace's corresponding counts are 2,035 and 4,070. No revision explanation is supplied. This does not change calculations within each case, but it prevents claims of demonstrated continuity across a real operating history.

These are source-grounded judgments of anonymous submitted narratives and proposed routing. Execution-complete flags do not prove external delivery, customer benefit, remediation success, or financial closure. This review supplies neither external peer review nor empirical enterprise proof, and makes no claims about unseen episodes or which implementation produced any output. No paid calls or code changes were made.
